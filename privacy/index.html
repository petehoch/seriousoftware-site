Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Seriousoftware respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Seriousoftware applications and services handle information.

FetchBag

FetchBag does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, contact information, or other personally identifying information.

The text, images, files, documents, and other content you store in FetchBag are not collected by or transmitted to Seriousoftware.

Subscriptions

FetchBag offers subscriptions through Apple's App Store. Apple processes purchases and payments. Seriousoftware does not receive or store your payment card information.

FetchBag uses RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and determine subscription status. RevenueCat receives purchase information associated with an anonymous identifier generated for the app. FetchBag does not provide RevenueCat with your name, email address, or other personally identifying information.

Purchase information is used only to provide and manage subscription functionality.

Third-Party Services

Subscription purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and are subject to Apple's privacy practices.

Apple Privacy Policy:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

RevenueCat processes subscription information on behalf of Seriousoftware.

RevenueCat Privacy Policy:
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

FetchBag does not use third-party advertising, does not track users across apps or websites, and does not use personal information for advertising or marketing.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our applications and services change. The current version will always be available on this page.
