FetchBag

Everything. Relevant. Connected.

Stop re-finding information you already have. FetchBag keeps the text snippets, images, documents, and links you reuse in one focused place, connected to the context that makes them meaningful. Instead of remembering where you put something, find it using what you do remember—the people, places, dates, tags, or details surrounding it. FetchBag helps you spend less time searching and more time using the information you already have.
